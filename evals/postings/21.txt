Weeknight Lemon Garlic Chicken Thighs

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

Ingredients
- 8 bone-in chicken thighs
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt and pepper
- A handful of fresh parsley

Method
1. Heat the oven to 220C. Pat the thighs dry and rub with oil, paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Sear skin-side down in an oven-safe pan for 6 minutes until golden.
3. Flip, add garlic and lemon zest, and roast for 20 minutes.
4. Finish with lemon juice and parsley. Rest for 5 minutes before serving with rice.

Tip: if you are looking for a fresh start in the kitchen, this one is hard to get wrong.
